# Bouclier website

Product, support and privacy pages for Bouclier, the ad blocker for Safari on Mac, iPhone and iPad.
Served by GitHub Pages at https://fablab503-collab.github.io/bouclier/

The pages are in English and French: the language follows the browser, and #fr / #en in the link
choose one. The images in img/ show Bouclier running in Safari, in Apple's product bezels (used
under Apple's marketing guidelines); they are made with tools/screenshots in the app's repository,
which also keeps a copy of these pages in site/.

The app itself is not in this repository.
