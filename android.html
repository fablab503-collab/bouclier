Install

  1. Download the file on your phone and open it.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then tap Install.
  3. Open Bouclier and tap the shield. Android asks once to allow a VPN connection: this is how Bouclier filters addresses, on your phone only.

What it does

  • Blocks ads, trackers and malicious sites in Chrome, in your news apps, in games, everywhere.
  • 90,000+ addresses from EasyList, EasyPrivacy, Liste FR and the Online Malicious URL Blocklist.
  • See what was blocked today, this week and all time; allow a site in one tap; pause for 1 hour, 2 hours or until tomorrow.
  • English and French. Starts again after a restart.

Privacy

Bouclier answers your phone's address lookups itself and blocks the ones that lead to ads, trackers and malware. It uses Android's VPN feature to do this, but nothing leaves your phone: no server, no account, no analytics, no data collection.

Limits

  • Blocking works at the address level, so empty ad spaces can remain inside pages.
  • Android allows one VPN at a time, so Bouclier cannot run together with another VPN app.

Why not Google Play?

Google Play does not allow apps that block ads in other apps. Bouclier is registered with Google's Android developer verification under its developer's verified identity, so it installs normally on certified Android phones.

  • Bloque pubs, traqueurs et sites malveillants dans Chrome, vos apps d’info, vos jeux, partout.

Confidentialité

Bouclier répond lui-même aux recherches d’adresses du téléphone et bloque celles qui mènent aux pubs, traqueurs et malwares. Il utilise la fonction VPN d’Android, mais rien ne quitte votre téléphone : pas de serveur, pas de compte, aucune statistique, aucune collecte de données.

  • Le blocage se fait au niveau des adresses : des espaces de pub vides peuvent rester dans les pages.
